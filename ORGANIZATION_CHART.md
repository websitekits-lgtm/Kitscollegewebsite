## 📋 Structured View

### **Level 1 — Apex**
- **GOVERNING BODY**

### **Level 2 — Leadership**
- **PRINCIPAL** (reports to Governing Body)

### **Level 3 — Functional Heads** (report to Principal)
Five branches lead from the Principal:

#### 1️⃣ HEAD OF THE DEPARTMENTS
Covers all academic departments:
- **CSE** — Computer Science & Engineering
- **AI & ML** — Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
- **CSE-AI** — Computer Science & Engineering (AI specialisation)
- **ECE** — Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **CIVIL** — Civil Engineering
- **MECHANICAL** — Mechanical Engineering
- **EEE** — Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Each department further splits into:
- **TEACHING** staff
- **NON-TEACHING** staff

#### 2️⃣ EXAM SECTION
- Controls all examination-related activities (internal & external)

#### 3️⃣ ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE
Reports to the Principal and oversees the following operational areas (cascading top-down):
1. **ACCOUNTS**
2. **ADMISSIONS**
3. **SCHOLARSHIPS**
4. **GENERAL MAINTENANCE**

#### 4️⃣ COMMITTEES
The institutional committees, including but not limited to:
- **NSS** — National Service Scheme
- **LIBRARY** Committee
- **SPORTS AND GAMES** Committee
- **WOMEN EMPOWERMENT** Committee
- **OTHER COMMITTEES**

#### 5️⃣ IQAC — Internal Quality Assurance Cell
